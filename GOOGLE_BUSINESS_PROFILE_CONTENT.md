## 8. Review engine (start after verification)

1. In your GBP dashboard, get your **review link**: Home → "Ask for reviews"
   (a short `g.page/r/...` link).
2. After every handover, send the client this WhatsApp message:

```
Hi [name]! It was a pleasure furnishing your home 🏠 If you're happy with the result, would you mind leaving us a short Google review? It takes 1 minute and helps our small team a lot: [your g.page review link]
```

3. Reply to **every** review within 48 hours (thank positives; address
   negatives calmly with an offer to fix).
4. When reviews accumulate, update the numbers in
   `src/components/StructuredData.tsx` (AGGREGATE_RATING) so the website
   schema matches your real rating.

**Target:** 2–4 reviews per month. Steady velocity beats a one-time burst.
