## 5. Q&A — post each question and answer yourself from the owner account

**Q: How much does it cost to furnish an apartment in Dubai?**
A: Essential packages start from AED 26,000 for a studio, AED 34,500 for a 1-bedroom, AED 46,000 for a 2-bedroom and AED 60,500 for a 3-bedroom (excluding VAT and appliances). Premium adds a living-room feature wall and Luxury upgrades the walls and styling throughout. Full prices: https://www.azizahomes.com/packages

**Q: How long does furnishing take?**
A: Most apartments take 7–20 days from confirmation and building access, depending on the package and layout. Villas are planned individually.

**Q: Can you furnish my apartment while I'm overseas?**
A: Yes. Design sign-off, progress updates, installation and a photo-documented handover all happen without you flying to Dubai.

**Q: Do you furnish Airbnb / holiday homes?**
A: Yes, with durable guest-proof materials, a complete setup and photo-ready styling for your listing. Details: https://www.azizahomes.com/holiday-home-furnishing-dubai

**Q: Which areas of Dubai do you cover?**
A: All of Dubai: Downtown, Marina, JBR, Business Bay, Palm Jumeirah, JVC, JLT, Dubai Hills, City Walk, Creek Harbour, Meydan, Arabian Ranches, Damac Hills and more.

**Q: Are appliances included?**
A: Package prices exclude appliances. We can quote an appliance set on request.

**Q: Are you related to Azizi Developments?**
A: No. Aziza Home is an independent interior design company. We do furnish apartments in Azizi buildings, like any other building in Dubai.

## 8. Reviews (start the day the profile is verified)

1. In the profile, tap **"Ask for reviews"** and copy the short review link.
2. Send to every past client now, then to each new client at handover:

```
Hi [name]! It was a pleasure furnishing your home 🏠 If you're happy with the result, would you leave us a short Google review? It takes a minute and helps our small team a lot: [review link]
```

3. Reply to **every** review within 48 hours.
4. Never offer discounts or gifts for reviews, and never write them yourselves. Google removes them and can suspend the profile.

**Target:** 20 reviews by mid-January 2027, then 2–4 a month.
